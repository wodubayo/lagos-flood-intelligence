# July 2024 Lagos flood: independent evidence review

## Purpose and status

This review supports the exploratory Phase 2 candidate audit. Reports establish event context at named locations; none of the sources below provides an independently verified, georeferenced flood boundary at the Sentinel-1 acquisition time of **2024-07-03 06:30:17 WAT (05:30:17 UTC)**. No accuracy score or pixel-level validation is warranted from these reports alone.

The user's QGIS screenshot places the visual review around Festac, Satellite Town, Trade Fair Complex and nearby waterways. This identifies a review neighborhood, not the coordinates of the previously highlighted patch. Candidate statistics remain study-area-wide.

These are separate publications, but some may repeat shared official statements or social-media observations. They must not be counted as independent pixel labels or assumed independent accounts of the same site. Article publication time is not the time a location became flooded.

## Interpretation for the selected pair

June 21 to July 3 includes intervening reported rainfall, including June 26. Therefore even a real water-related radar change cannot automatically be assigned to the July 3 morning storm alone. News descriptions of early rainfall make acquisition-time flooding plausible but do not demonstrate it at the review patch.

The July 7 retrospective strengthens the case for investigating Satellite Town and Amuwo-Odofin; it does not validate the candidate footprint. No absence of a news report is interpreted as evidence of a dry location.

## Required validation evidence

Before selecting a final rule or claiming detection accuracy, obtain dated observations with usable coordinates or mapped extents and a timing relationship to the overpass. Record location precision, observation time/timezone, original source and uncertainty. Include independently established dry as well as flooded locations, with evaluation samples kept separate from threshold tuning.

Until then, retain the label **exploratory backscatter-change candidates**. Terrain and radar consistency are diagnostic information, not independent flood validation.
